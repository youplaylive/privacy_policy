# Privacy Policy — Number Drop

_Last updated: [DATE]_

[DEVELOPER NAME] ("we") built Number Drop as a free game with no
advertisements. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information we collect

**None.** Number Drop does not collect, transmit, sell or share any
personal information or usage data.

- There are no accounts, registration or logins.
- There are no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
- The app does not request network access and works fully offline.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores your game progress, high scores, statistics and settings
locally on your device. This data never leaves the device, except through
your operating system's own device backup (for example Android Auto Backup
or an iCloud device backup) if you have that turned on. That backup is
governed by Google's or Apple's terms. You can delete the data at any time
with **Settings → Reset progress**, or by uninstalling the app.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including
children under 13.
